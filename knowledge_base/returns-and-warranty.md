# Returns and warranty
## Returns
All orders go through Amazon, so returns do too. The customer starts a return in Your Orders on Amazon within 30 days of delivery. Amazon sends the return label and handles the refund.
We cannot start, speed up, or approve a return or refund from our side. A colleague answers every refund question.
## Warranty
- E-bikes: 2 years on the frame, the motor, and the controller. 1 year on the display, lights, charger, and other parts.
- Batteries: 1 year.
The warranty covers manufacturing faults. It does not cover crashes, water damage from submersion, normal wear such as brake pads and tires, or use of third-party batteries and chargers.
## Other products
For any other EBikes IL product, the same rules apply: returns through Amazon within 30 days, and a 1-year warranty on manufacturing faults.
