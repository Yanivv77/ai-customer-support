# Batteries
## Models and compatibility
- BAT-36-10: 36 V, 10 Ah. Fits the Carmel C1 and the Carmel F1.
- BAT-36-14: 36 V, 14 Ah. Fits the Carmel C1 only. It gives about 40 percent more range.
We support only original batteries and the original charger CH-36. Third-party batteries and chargers are not covered by the warranty and may be unsafe.
## Charging
- Use the original charger only.
- Charge at room temperature, on a hard surface, away from curtains and other things that burn.
- Do not leave a battery charging while nobody is home.
- A full charge takes 4 to 5 hours.
- If the battery was in a hot car, let it cool down before charging.
## Normal behavior
- Range drops in cold or very hot weather, with headwinds, and with a heavy load.
- The battery feels slightly warm right after a ride or a charge.
- The charger light turns green when the battery is full.
## Battery will not hold a charge
First check that the original charger is used and that the battery clicks in fully. If the range is still much lower than before, ask for the order number, the battery model, and how many months ago the battery was bought. A colleague takes over.
## Battery safety
This message is fixed. Send it word for word, and then a colleague takes over. Never add other advice about charging, repair, or disposal.
Applies when a battery is swollen, hot while not in use, leaking, smoking, or smells of chemicals or burning.
English: Please stop using the battery and do not charge it. If it is safe to do so, put it outside on a surface that does not burn, away from buildings. If there is smoke or fire, call 102 right away. A colleague will contact you.
עברית: הפסיקו להשתמש בסוללה ואל תטענו אותה. אם זה בטוח, הניחו אותה בחוץ על משטח שאינו נדלק, הרחק ממבנים. אם יש עשן או אש, התקשרו מיד למספר 102. נציג שלנו ייצור איתכם קשר.
