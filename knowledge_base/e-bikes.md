- S: 150 to 164 cm.
- M: 165 to 179 cm.
- L: 180 to 195 cm.
A rider on the border between two sizes should take the smaller frame for city riding. The F1 has one size and fits riders from 150 to 190 cm.

## Display error codes
- E10: the battery is not detected. Switch the bike off, take the battery out, push it back in until it clicks, and switch on again.
- E21: the speed sensor has no signal. Switch the bike off and check that the sensor cable near the rear wheel is plugged in tight.
- E30: the controller is too hot. Stop riding, switch the bike off, and wait 15 minutes.
If the code comes back after these steps, ask for the order number, the model, and the code, then a colleague takes over.
## Squeaking brakes
New brake pads need bedding in. Ride at a moderate speed and brake firmly to a near stop about 20 times. Clean the brake disc with isopropyl alcohol on a clean cloth.
If a brake feels weak, or the lever touches the handlebar, the customer must stop riding. A colleague handles it.
