# Shipping
## Orders
Amazon ships every order. Delivery dates and tracking are in Your Orders on Amazon. We cannot see or change an Amazon delivery.
## Replacement parts
Replacement parts ship from our own warehouse, free of charge. They leave within 2 business days and arrive in 3 to 7 business days. Business days are Sunday to Thursday. The customer receives a tracking link by email when the parcel leaves.
A replacement battery is always arranged by a colleague, because batteries need special handling.
## Countries
We ship within Israel. Replacement parts go to the same address as the original order.
